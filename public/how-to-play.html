How to play DoodleOnline

DoodleOnline is a multiplayer drawing party game for 2–20 people. Create a private room, share its code with friends, choose a mode in the lobby, and start once everyone is ready.

Three simple steps

  1. Write a short phrase or respond to the prompt.
  2. Draw what you see, or describe another player's drawing.
  3. Reveal the resulting stories together at the end.

Seven game modes

Drawing Telephone

Alternate between phrases and drawings.

Quick Sketch

A faster version of the classic chain, with half the time.

Masterpiece

Take more time to create each drawing.

From Memory

Look at a drawing for seven seconds, then recreate it from memory.

In Motion

Add frames to a shared animation.

Creative Copy

Redraw each reference in your own style.

Icebreaker

Play two quick rounds: one phrase and one drawing.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

Guests can join using a nickname. The game may create an anonymous technical session to keep the room working.

Do I have to enable my camera?

No. Camera and microphone are optional and require browser permission.

Can I play alone?

Yes. Use the solo canvas practice option on the home page.

How do I invite friends?

Create a room and share its invitation link or room code. Rooms are intended for invited players.
