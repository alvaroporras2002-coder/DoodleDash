Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026. This page describes known application behavior and must be reviewed by the site operator against the production configuration before publication for advertising.

Information used by the game

The game uses a nickname, chosen avatar, anonymous authentication/session identifiers, room membership, gameplay actions, prompts, drawings and saved drafts to provide multiplayer sessions. Local browser storage may remember your nickname, avatar, room and invitation information.

Service providers

Supabase supports authentication and multiplayer game data. Optional audio/video calls may use a real-time communications service. The exact providers, data retention, regions and subprocessors must be confirmed against the deployed environment.

Camera and microphone

Camera and microphone are optional. Browser permission is requested when you choose to use the call feature. Do not enable them if you do not want to share audio or video with other participants.

Advertising and cookies

Advertising is not enabled by this policy page. If Google AdSense is activated, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, subject to applicable consent requirements. The operator must configure consent and update this disclosure before ads are served.

Your choices

You may decline camera and microphone permissions and clear local browser storage. For questions about access, deletion or retention of server-side data, use the contact page. A private contact channel and verified retention policy must be added before production advertising.
