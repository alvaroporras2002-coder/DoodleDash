# DoodleOnline AdSense launch checklist

Public pages added: How to play, About, Privacy, Terms, Contact. These are initial drafts, not verified legal compliance.

Before requesting review:
- Publish a private, working contact address and verify privacy disclosures, data retention, vendor names and deletion requests.
- Confirm production domain and use it for canonical URLs and sitemap.xml.
- Register and verify the site with AdSense. Add publisher ID only after issuance; never invent one.
- Create /ads.txt with the exact authorized publisher line provided by AdSense.
- Configure a Google-certified CMP when required for EEA, UK and Swiss visitors, and confirm consent behavior.
- Keep ads away from gameplay, drawing controls, room invitations and private audio/video communication.
- Add permanent footer links from the SPA to all five pages; test on mobile and desktop.
- Verify live public pages, build, gameplay and accessibility; check Google policy compliance and request review.

No ads or tracking are activated by this PR. Approval is Google's decision.
